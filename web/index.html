About

Who we are

Knics is a personal AI console that helps you talk to an agent, run schedules, manage email, and keep your day on track — all tied to your own account.

We build tools that stay useful without getting in the way: clear conversation, reliable reminders, and settings that belong to you.

Legal

Privacy

We use your Google account only to sign you in and identify your workspace. Conversation history, schedules, email settings, and usage stay associated with your account.

Secrets such as mailbox app passwords are stored on the server for your account and are not kept in the browser after you save. We do not sell your personal data.

You can sign out at any time. For privacy questions, use the Contact us page.
